Common Questions

Everything You Want To Know
Before Moving Forward.

These are the questions every serious operator asks before requesting a territory review. Read through them — then check if your market is still open.

Do I need a medical license to run a Got Stem Cells territory?

No. Dr. Sean Arora is a Board-Certified MD licensed across all 50 states. His physician oversight, clinical protocols, and medical authority are built into every territory license at no additional cost. You do not need a medical license, a clinical background, or your own independent physician relationship to operate.

What does the $100,000 founding license fee include?

The founding license includes: an exclusive protected territory, the dinner seminar system (scripts, event deck, venue playbook, promotion framework), Ava AI (24/7 SMS, voice, video intake, web chat follow-up), full CRM and pipeline automation, a market-ready brand presence and landing page, patient education assets, Dr. Arora's medical oversight, preferred stem cell pricing through Precision Biologics, and launch and performance support through your first events. The price increases to $250K once onboarding documentation is finalized.

How is this different from a traditional franchise?

A traditional franchise charges 8–12% ongoing royalties, requires mandatory marketing fund contributions of 2–4%, and imposes strict operational compliance. The Got Stem Cells? license is a flat fee — no ongoing royalties, no mandatory fund, and full operational freedom. You own the business, you keep the economics, and you control the market strategy. It's structured to give you the support of a franchise model without the control loss or ongoing drag.

What are the ongoing costs after the license fee?

Ongoing costs include stem cell product costs (accessed at preferred pricing through Precision Biologics), NP or clinical costs for treatment delivery logistics, event promotion and marketing spend, and standard business operations. The ~40% net margin figure in the model accounts for all of these hard costs at target run rate — meaning a territory producing $1M+ annually is projected to net approximately $400K after expenses.

How long does it take to run my first dinner seminar?

Most operators go from approved territory to first live dinner seminar in 60–90 days. The rollout is structured: weeks 1–2 cover territory approval and strategy, weeks 3–4 cover platform setup (website, CRM, Ava AI workflows), weeks 5–6 launch demand generation and event promotion, and weeks 7–8 deliver the first live education event. Launch and performance support continues through the first event cycle.

What revenue can I realistically expect from this model?

Dinner seminars average $20,000–$80,000 in revenue per event at an approximately 50% attendee-to-consultation conversion rate. At target run rate — with consistent event frequency, strong follow-up, and market development — territories are designed to produce $1M+ in annual revenue. Individual results vary based on market, operator execution, event frequency, follow-up quality, and local compliance environment. The revenue calculator on this page lets you model your specific scenario.

How many territories are available in my state?

Depending on state population, there are 3–6 protected territories available per state. Some markets are already claimed — once a territory is approved and assigned, it is no longer available to another operator. Territory availability is confirmed during the review process. The first step is submitting a territory inquiry so we can check whether your preferred market is still open before walking through the full model.

Do I need clinical or medical experience to run a territory?

No. The system is designed for business operators — chiropractors who want to expand their revenue model, med spa owners looking for a premium add-on, entrepreneurs entering the health and wellness space, and multi-location operators building market share. You do not personally deliver treatments. The clinical structure, NP-led treatment logistics, and physician oversight are part of the platform. Your role is running the business: filling the room, managing the follow-up system, and building local market authority.

How does stem cell sourcing work, and what is Precision Biologics?

Precision Biologics is the stem cell supply and distribution company founded by Seth Berge — the same infrastructure that has powered 100+ clinics nationwide. Got Stem Cells? territory operators access stem cell products at preferred pricing through Precision Biologics, meaning you're sourcing from the same supply chain that the broader network depends on. This is a significant cost advantage over operators who have to source independently at retail pricing.

What happens after I submit a territory inquiry?

The process is straightforward. Step one: we confirm whether your preferred market is still open. Step two: we review operator fit — market goals, capital, involvement level, and whether this model makes sense for your situation. Step three: we walk through the full platform — seminar engine, Ava AI, CRM, clinical structure, support, and launch path. Step four: if the market and model are a fit, we discuss next steps. If not, you walk away with a clear picture of what the opportunity looks like. No pressure, no obligation at the inquiry stage.
